# Union Square street details

Original Blender-authored street-name assemblies, newspaper dispensers and bicycle racks for Whimsical SF. Generated by `tools/blender/build_square_street_details.py`; editable source: `art/source/square-street-details.blend`.

The green blades carry the actual crossing street names. Their proportions and construction are a practical interpretation of San Francisco street furniture, not a claim to reproduce an engineering drawing. Newspaper cover text and skyline graphics are original fictional artwork. No third-party photographs, textures or font files are included in the runtime asset.

Placement follows the project's reference map audit in `art/references/union-square-layout.md` (street relationships from OpenStreetMap, © OpenStreetMap contributors). The props occupy pedestrian space around the course and are scenery, not driving obstacles.

## Coordinate contract

This asset uses the corrected, right-handed world: **X east, Y up, Z south**. The authoring script accepts semantic north coordinates, places them on Blender's +Y, and exports ordinary Y-up glTF. Positive semantic north therefore becomes **negative exported Z**.

Load with an identity rotation and scale, translated by `(0, 22, 0)`. Do not put this model below a legacy Z-reflection group. The geometry already has the intended handedness. Street and newspaper lettering remains in separate mesh nodes with `sfLettering: true` extras and a proper rotation basis. Text is readable from each sign face without a negative scale.

All placement coordinates, draw counts and triangle counts are in `square-street-details.meta.json`. The loader `src/square-street-details.ts` supplies readiness, shadow-quality control and distance culling. Attach it only after the corrected world orientation is applied.
