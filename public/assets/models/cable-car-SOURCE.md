# Powell combination cable car

Original model authored for Whimsical SF in Blender 4.5 LTS, September 2026. No third-party meshes, photographs, logos, or texture pixels are included. Public photographs and a museum drawing were consulted for proportions and construction. This is an artistic game interpretation, not an engineering reconstruction or an exact replica of a particular fleet car.

References inspected:

- [Cable Car Museum — Powell Street Cable Car](https://www.cablecarmuseum.org/car-powell.html): side elevation, front/three-quarter photograph, dimensions and fleet description. The museum identifies the real vehicle as 27′6″ long, 8′ wide, 10′4″ high. Its elevation is the source for the single open grip end, four-window rear saloon, narrow boarding platform and raised clerestory.
- [Cable Car Museum — Cable Cars](https://www.cablecarmuseum.org/cablecars.html): distinguishes the single-ended Powell combination car from the double-ended California car. The model follows the Powell arrangement.
- [SFMTA — Cable Cars](https://www.sfmta.com/getting-around/muni/cable-cars): public front photograph informed the oxblood enamel, warm painted timber, brass headlamp and hand-lettered route-board treatment. Liveries vary; this model combines that palette with the Powell body.

The course's established obstacle is 2.25 m wide by 7.2 m long. The asset keeps that footprint and compresses real dimensions slightly; it does not change obstacle physics. Front is +Z, up is +Y, rail contact is Y=0. The game places and tilts the complete asset using the existing route frame.

Included details: curved roof and clerestory, individually modeled varnished bench slats, saloon interior benches, glazed windows with separate timber sashes and latches, brake/grip controls, hanging bell and pull, brass arches and poles, hand loops, rear lattice guards, running boards, flanged rail wheels, axle covers, bogie springs, rivets, raised enamel panels and original geometry lettering. Materials are grouped into seven mesh batches. A small original wood-grain texture is embedded in the GLB; its PNG is also provided for editing.
