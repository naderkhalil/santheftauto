# Map geometry and finish audit — 2026-09-25

## Corrections

The neighborhood generator previously placed ornament on the survey's projected
frontage plane. Concave, recessed and angled footprints could leave that plane
several metres outside a wall. Cornices, window frames and foundation trim then
appeared as detached frames. Frontages now use real footprint edges with their
own midpoint, outward normal and length. The individually authored Hayes
restaurant shell retains its explicit matching facade.

Rebuilt all 1,558 included neighborhood buildings in the editable Blender source
and exported model. The distant model already follows physical footprint edges
and regenerated byte-for-byte identically. Detailed geometry fell from 3,016,243
to 2,438,247 triangles; the GLB fell from 36,325,344 to 31,331,788 bytes.

The Ferry finish previously clamped speed and progress even when an earlier
ordered gate was missing, leaving an unfinished race stuck at the endpoint.
It now returns the player to the last earned gate, preserves elapsed time and
statistics, and explains the return. Valid races still finish exactly once.
Untimed exploration explicitly bypasses race-gate requirements. Gate crossings
also tolerate floating-point error when landing exactly on the finish plane.

## City Loop release pass — 2026-09-30

Union Square shortcuts now count as valid routes to the first Powell checkpoint.
The former Stockton/Geary/Powell approach gates are removed; later checkpoints
remain ordered. Personal bests use a new storage key for the changed race rules.

Holding brake now reverses throughout grounded City Loop driving. Road walls
account for the rotated car footprint, and parked traffic remains solid on
repeated approaches. Continuous contact counts as one collision. Q, the on-screen
Unstick button, the pause menu, and controller Y/Triangle recover onto nearby
clear pavement while preserving elapsed time and earned progress. Recovery stays
before an unearned checkpoint and before a launch when necessary.

Gold road-edge rails and outer district barriers mark the driving limits without
closing plaza shortcuts. The chase camera faces the direction of travel when
reversing and shortens near plaza solids and road edges.

Validation: 142 gameplay tests pass, including complete City Loop runs with parked
traffic at 30/60/120 Hz, repeated collisions, reverse wall escape, shortcut finish,
recovery, and sampled camera clearance. Browser checks cover on-screen, keyboard,
and pause recovery, plus the 44px recovery target at a 390px-wide viewport.
Physical controller testing and first-time-player playtesting remain release checks.
