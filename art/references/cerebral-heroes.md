# Cerebral Valley landmark architecture

These are original Blender models guided by inspected photographs, OpenStreetMap building outlines, and the USGS 3DEP elevation grid. They are not scans or measured architectural surveys. The source remains editable in `art/source/cerebral-heroes.blend`; `tools/blender/build_cerebral_heroes.py` reproduces both browser assets and the architectural reviews.

## Photographic references inspected on September 24, 2026

- **Painted Ladies, 710–720 Steiner Street:** [Carol M. Highsmith, Library of Congress](https://www.loc.gov/item/2013630348/). The photographic preview was inspected through Google Images; the Library of Congress page itself presented a security verification screen. Other visible close views corroborated the six separate gables, asymmetric canted bays, recessed right entrances, tall stairs, carved horizontal cornices, and individual cream, sage, yellow/coral, tan, blue, and pale olive palettes. The broader **722 Steiner** neighbor has its own rounded oriel, conical turret, and hipped roof. It is not a seventh copy of the six gabled houses.
- **531–535 Page Street:** [Zumper property page](https://www.zumper.com/apartment-buildings/p675594/531-535-page-street-lower-haight-san-francisco-ca). The exterior photograph identified for that exact address shows three stories, two projecting square bays, tall paired windows, pale taupe walls, and a notched flat cornice. The neighboring sage house with a triangular gable is a different property. Mixed unrelated property photographs in general search results were excluded.
- **Café Réveille, 201 Steiner:** [Eater SF, Patricia Chang exterior photography](https://sf.eater.com/2018/2/23/17046478/cafe-reveille-lower-haight-steiner-open-bean-there). The original article photograph was opened and inspected. It shows tall white storefront piers, broad dark lower glazing, white divided transoms, a narrow corner entry, taupe upper bays, a pink sign with black lettering, a pink sidewalk board, white rectangular grass planters, thin black café chairs, small round tables, heaters, and string lights.
- **Absinthe, 398 Hayes:** [Official restaurant site](https://www.absinthe.com/) exterior previews and visible street photographs were inspected. The distinguishing elements are the pale upper bays, deep green storefront, rounded corner oriel, segmented frosted-glass fan canopy, and green script wordmark. Its doorway belongs at the southwest corner of its building: Hayes is south and Gough is west.

- **450 Hayes / a Mano:** [Handel Architects' project documentation](https://handelarchitects.com/project/450-hayes). The original exterior photographs and written design description were inspected directly. This is a four-story building with vertical warm wood panels, graphite metal surrounds, irregular projecting and angled bay windows, and ground-floor retail. It is not a white Victorian apartment block. The two wings are connected by bridges over an internal courtyard. The actual Hayes frontage is the 39.75 m south edge of the irregular OSM footprint; the generic 61 m overall diagonal width would have placed the façade outside its parcel.

## Placement and export contract

The authoritative placement source is `art/references/cerebral-valley-layout.json`, generated from the live OpenStreetMap API and USGS 3DEP. Reference origin: longitude **−122.42415**, latitude **37.77655**. Blender uses **X east, Y north, Z elevation**. Standard glTF export becomes **X east, Y elevation, Z south**. Attach these assets directly to the unreflected Three.js scene. Ground elevations are already included; do not add a second course height offset.

The Painted Ladies face west toward Alamo Square. 531 Page faces north toward Page Street. Réveille occupies the northwest corner of Waller and Steiner, with its primary frontage facing east onto Steiner and its long return facing south onto Waller. Absinthe faces south onto Hayes with the corner entrance at Gough on its west end. Each of the eleven reserved OSM building IDs is recorded in `heroes.meta.json`.

## Modeling and practical limits

The hero fronts use separate sash, projecting sills, canted bay planes, porch columns, spindle railings, stepped entrances, horizontal weatherboards, bracketed cornices, gable ornament, pitched roofs, and chimney caps. The storefronts have physical frames, furniture, planters, signage, and their distinctive corner structures. Roughness and fine tangent-normal maps are original, packed PBR textures; no photographic pixels were reused as façade textures.

The geometry is batched by landmark and material so the renderer can cull individual properties. Draco compression and a separate decimated LOD keep downloads and rendering moderate. Roofs, backs, and returns are present so the properties remain coherent from the route’s multiple directions. Roof/back details, floor heights not present in public data, and small decorative measurements are interpretations. Some rear wings are simplified to the enclosing parcel dimensions. The main photographs are from different years, so paint colors and shop fittings represent those references rather than a verified same-day street survey.

All 2,408 sampled glyph faces across property numbers, café signs, Absinthe, and 450 Hayes/a Mano passed the one-metre outward clearance ray test with zero blocked samples.
