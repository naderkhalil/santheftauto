# Whimsical SF

A San Francisco postcard that learned to fly. The city should be recognizable from its streets, building proportions and public spaces before the car ever leaves the ground. Keep the saturated sunlight and playful glides, and spend detail on the places people remember.

## The visual language

Warm limestone and granite, brick and painted plaster, blue window reflections, green roof tiles, red Chinatown lanterns, coral bodywork and turquoise bay water. Color comes from distinct materials and daylight. Human-scale doors, windows, paving joints, café furniture and cornices give the larger city its scale.

Union Square is an open terraced plaza, with the slender Dewey Monument above granite stairs, retaining walls, seven date palms, four broad jacaranda canopies, planted beds, corner heart sculptures and red umbrellas. The center is raised above the perimeter; a higher garden terrace marks the Post side. East and west café pavilions and the southeast subway entrance give the edges distinct uses. These are interpreted elevations and plantings for this course, not a botanical or dimensional survey.

The Westin's historic wings and recessed courts face the square from Powell, with modern towers behind. Macy's and the white marble I. Magnin wing anchor Geary. Apple faces south across Post at the Stockton end, with its public garden and the Grand Hyatt behind it; Saks, Tiffany and the former Williams-Sonoma building complete that street wall. Neiman Marcus and its visible rotunda sit southeast across Geary, while five smaller Stockton parcels are interrupted by Maiden Lane. The outside corners have occupied hotel and retail frontage. A sign alone cannot establish a landmark: its correct parcel, silhouette, neighboring buildings and approach view must agree.

Geography must also survive the camera projection. Facing north, Apple belongs on the right of Saks; facing east from the Westin, Apple is on the left and Neiman Marcus on the right. The original positive-north scene coordinates produced a mirrored view. The renderer now converts the map into a physically oriented world, while the route retains its established coordinates. Clockwise turns, steering, signs and asymmetric model details must agree with that correction.

Powell is a cable-car climb framed by masonry buildings. Grant Avenue is a narrower Chinatown corridor: Dragon Gate at the entrance, varied small shopfronts, bilingual signs, balconies, lantern strings and green tile roofs. The skyline and waterfront should appear in believable directions. Building heights, footprints and landmark relationships carry more weight than adding generic towers.

A wide chase camera retains a gentle fisheye character, while the center remains clear for driving. Preserve bright sky, dry asphalt and warm stone without washing out material colors.

## The route

The approximately 2.33 km course laps Union Square along **Post, Stockton, Geary and Powell**, continues up **Powell**, turns along **Bush** into **Grant Avenue**, then follows **Washington** toward the **Embarcadero** and **Market Street / Ferry Building** finish.

This is a compressed interpretation of those street connections. Block spacing, rounded turns, architecture and hill heights are authored for the game, not surveyed geography. The more grounded street layout makes the three deliberate departures into flight feel special.

Three hill glides occur on Powell, Grant and Washington. Their flight paths carry the uphill tangent into a gentle arc and return to the road smoothly. The coral coupe's four wheels pivot ninety degrees into cyan hover pods, slide outward and deploy fins. Nine gold sun rings indicate the flight line and refill boost. Wheels return before touchdown, followed by a small suspension response.

Speed of at least 24 m/s (about 54 mph) triggers a launch. Steering remains active in flight; boost adds lift and braking dives within the flight corridor. Slower drivers can remain on the road. Street corners guide the car along the route while allowing lane choice, braking and drifting. This is approachable arcade driving and flight.

## The waterfront payoff

Market Street opens onto the Ferry Building on a pier over animated turquoise water, with the silver Bay Bridge spanning the background. A smooth camera reveal and compact results keep that composition visible. Ferries and sailboats give the bay movement. The automatic tour also ends here, and its **The Bay** button previews the finish.

## Implemented Union Square art pass

The first Blender stretch established gray rusticated Westin masonry, deep window openings, bronze entries, copper cornices and separate rear towers; the Dewey Monument with modeled Victory, bronze globe, wreath and trident; mature date palms; and a museum-reference Powell combination cable car.

The wider square pass carries that asset workflow into Post, Geary, Stockton and the missing exterior corners. Apple's silver portal and large sliding glass panes expose its tables, stairs and mezzanine; its rear garden leads toward the correctly placed Hyatt. Macy's glass atrium and I. Magnin marble contrast with the patterned Neiman Marcus stone and modeled rotunda. The Stockton buildings have different rooflines, deep shop windows and complete returns around Maiden Lane. The plaza now has more pronounced terraces, planted retaining edges, ramps, railings, benches, cafes and a visible subway entrance.

The newest architectural expansion makes those street walls into continuous blocks. North-side hotels have complete side and rear elevations toward Sutter; the southwest hotel frontage continues toward O'Farrell. Macy's has a deeper terracotta rear body and Gothic parapet, while the Stockton boutiques extend toward Grant on both sides of Maiden Lane. Cornices wrap the corners; roofs have parapets, service courts and equipment. These views matter when the car turns or lifts above street level. Four crossing-street sign assemblies, two newspaper dispensers with original fictional covers and three galvanized bicycle racks are also authored in Blender for the close driving view.

The architecture is modeled originally from public photographic, museum and geographic references, then exported to GLB. Four plaza canopy trees reuse the separately credited CC0 jacaranda asset. Proportions remain adapted to the compressed course; geography is documented in [the reference map](art/references/union-square-layout.md). Blender review renders establish material and modeling checks, while final visual decisions belong in the moving game camera. The Westin is gray stone rather than generic warm sandstone; bright color comes from sky, copper, palms, flags, café umbrellas, storefronts and the car.

Window reflections are captured in the relevant district. Shadows cover the plaza as a coherent space; normal maps, bevels and contact shading support close material depth. The Westin and both new frontage sets have distant models. Seven palms share six instanced draws; four jacarandas share the scanned tree's geometry and materials. The **The Westin** postcard stop frames the building across the plaza, and the opening composition places it behind the monument and mature palms.

The latest city-completion pass replaces the vacant Powell/Bush lots and isolated downtown blocks with 169 connected building parcels across 41 blocks. Ground-floor shops follow the hill, while horizontal upper floors carry varied masonry, window reveals, projecting bays, cornices and selected fire escapes. All four sides and roofs are finished for turns and aerial views. Seven-metre public sidewalks, small planted street trees, corner crossings and paved service alleys replace the exposed beige ground; cross streets share the scanned asphalt of the race. This is original procedural neighborhood infill; it does not claim surveyed building-by-building accuracy.

The strongest individually authored fidelity remains around Union Square. The wider city, Dragon Gate, Grant storefronts, ordinary vehicles and people still have room for further art work. Keep the route and collisions in code and evaluate new assets from a moving driver's camera. [BLENDER_ASSETS.md](BLENDER_ASSETS.md) describes the source and export contract.
